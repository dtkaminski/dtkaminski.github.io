Finish the modal sweep: two radii and a dead fallback

Checked all five modals for what the business-event one had. Most of it was already
clean.

The four class-based modals share .modal / .modal-bg in greta-dashboard.html, and those
rules were right all along: var(--bg-card), var(--border-default), var(--r-lg) which
maps to --radius-none, and var(--shadow-lg) which maps to --shadow-raised — the same
token I reached for by hand in 356e4f1. The drawer is clean on the same three. The
marketing-os embed has no modals and no hardcoded radius or shadow at all.

Three things left:

- A textarea in the connect-a-source modal at borderRadius:6. It is an input, so
  --radius-md, same as the seven controls in 356e4f1.
- An inline <code> chip at borderRadius:4. Neither a container nor a control, so
  --radius-sm (2px) rather than --radius-md (5px) — numerically --radius-md is the
  closer of the two, but 5px on a chip with 1px 5px of padding reads as a pill, and
  --radius-sm is the step the system provides for small inline things.
- background:'var(--surface-1,#15151c)' on the business-event modal. 0e9dafc fixed that
  modal by defining --surface-1, so the fallback is now unreachable — but leaving the
  literal in place means deleting one alias silently returns a black dialog at 1.01:1
  contrast. The fallback is gone; the var stands on its own.

The six other --surface-* references keep their fallbacks, because those fall back to
PAL.panel, which is the right colour rather than a dark-theme ghost.

Also verified there is no unresolved custom property left anywhere: 125 defined across
the five stylesheets, 115 referenced across those plus greta-app.jsx, zero unresolved.
That class of bug is closed, not just this instance of it.

The one remaining dark hex in the file, #141414 in the PAL map, stays: PAL exists to
give SVG attributes concrete values, because SVG cannot resolve var().
