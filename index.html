FAQ

The questions a sceptical DTC founder asks

Is it self-serve, or done-for-you?

Both. Create your account and we help you connect Shopify: during early access we onboard brands one at a time, because a shop connection that fails quietly is worse than none. From then Greta runs on your data every hour by itself. The Managed tier (£750/mo) adds a weekly review of Greta's read with Dan. Paid self-serve plans are not live yet.

So is it AI, or is it Dan typing things in Word?

The platform. Every figure, ranking and action on the board is worked out by rules from your own data, every hour, with no person in between. An AI model writes the plain-English summary on Today and answers Ask Greta, and every figure it writes is checked against the data it was given. On the Managed tier Dan also reviews the read with you each week.

What does Greta actually do each day?

Every hour it works out, from your orders (they arrive every 30 minutes), ad spend (every 2 hours), site visits and emails, where your profit went against a typical month, and ranks what is worth doing by how sure it is and what it is worth. Each action names the product, ad or email, the £ a month or once, and the first step. When you mark one done, Greta checks later whether the promise held. What it does not do: make ads, write emails or change your store. It is read-only.

How is this different from Triple Whale?

Triple Whale answers "where did my last sale come from?" — an attribution question. We answer "what's actually true about my business, and what should I do this week?" — a synthesis question. Triple Whale charges £450+/mo for the attribution debate. Greta covers everything else: whether your site turns visits into orders, what to reorder, which emails earn, what your ads really bring in on your own orders, and one ranked list of what to do. The two tools don't compete on the same axis — Triple Whale lives in the "observe" layer. We live in the "act" layer.

What does "intellectually honest" actually mean in practice?

Things you can check in the product: (1) every figure says how sure Greta is: measured, likely, probably or possible; (2) sale weeks are kept out of "a typical month", so a sale ending does not read as a collapse; (3) a broken tracking feed is named on the page it affects, with what it makes less certain; (4) the forecast hides itself when it runs off course; (5) profit says when a missing cost makes it read high, and by how much; (6) Klaviyo's and the ad platforms' own claims are shown as claims, beside what your orders say.

What data sources do I need?

Shopify, Meta, Google Ads, GA4, and Klaviyo at minimum. Instagram organic and Microsoft Clarity add additional surface. Each one connects with its own sign-in; we connect Shopify with you during early access, then go through Greta's first read together. Until Shopify approves Greta's public listing, Greta starts from your last 58 days of orders, and the rest of your history follows once it does. TikTok, Amazon and Xero are not connected yet.

What if my data is messy?

This is the point. On the design partner's data, three of the four headline "crises" the team had been chasing turned out to be data artefacts — gifting orders contaminating the discount rate, wholesale revenue inflating the headline, draft orders polluting AOV. We segment first, surface findings second. Most platforms do the opposite.

How does the £/month figure get calculated?

Every action has a calculation against your own data, with the basis shown on the row, and says whether it is a month or once. A confidence label (measured, likely, probably, possible) tells you how much weight to put on the number. We are deliberately conservative — the total is illustrative, not additive (some findings overlap, e.g. site CVR work overlaps with specific JS fixes). The point isn't the precise total; it's that every recommendation has a £ tag rather than vague "this is important."

I already pay for Triple Whale. Why would I switch?

Most operators don't switch — they add. Triple Whale stays as the attribution debate venue for the media buyer. Greta sits next to it as the place you decide what to do this week.

What's the catch?

Three things: (1) we are new: Greta was built on one live design-partner brand during 2026, and we onboard new brands one at a time; (2) it advises, it does not act: you or your team make each change; (3) we do not do attribution modelling. If that is the only thing you care about, stay with Triple Whale.

Is my data safe?

Multi-tenant Supabase with row-level security, so each brand sees only its own data. We never train models on your data. Data is read-only — we don't write back to Meta, Shopify, or Klaviyo from this platform. The dashboard is a synthesis layer on top of your existing sources, not a system of record.
